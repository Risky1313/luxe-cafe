Drink photo prompts

One ChatGPT prompt for each of the 77 Luxe Café Pro recipes. Each one describes the exact glass, layers and garnish from its recipe.

  1. Tap Copy prompt on a drink and paste it into ChatGPT.
  2. Download the picture. Tap the file name under the drink to copy it, and save the picture with that name.
  3. Tick the circle so you know it's done. Your ticks stay on this device.

If a picture comes out wrong, like the wrong glass, an extra garnish or any writing, reply in ChatGPT with "Redo it, but…" and the fix. Start a fresh ChatGPT chat every 10 or so drinks so details from earlier pictures don't creep in.
